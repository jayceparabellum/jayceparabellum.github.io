## Role summary
Peraton Inc. (formerly Perspecta), remote / Herndon, VA, December 2019 to August 2025, nearly six years. Jayce started as a Systems Engineer III (Tier-2 AWS systems engineer) under Perspecta and grew into Cloud Systems Architect, Lead Associate after Perspecta became part of Peraton. Jayce worked on NBIS, the National Background Investigation Services platform of the Defense Counterintelligence and Security Agency (DCSA), which consolidates federal personnel vetting and background investigation processing for millions of applicants and cleared personnel.

## NBIS cloud architecture
Architected secure multi-cloud infrastructure for modernization of the NBIS platform under a $500M federal contract, supporting processing for millions of personnel security investigations. Managed RHEL, Amazon Linux and Ubuntu systems in AWS GovCloud, including network ACLs and infrastructure configuration hardening.

Ran quarterly STIG implementations using SCAP scanning, and wrote up the remediation or justification for every open finding for the cyber team.

## Deployment reliability
Implemented blue/green deployment in AWS GovCloud, cutting failed releases by 90% and bringing mean time to recovery (MTTR) down to 120 minutes.
