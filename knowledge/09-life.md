## Community
Jayce lives in Denver, Colorado. Jayce is a member of the LoDo Toastmasters club in Denver, where Jayce earned the Ice Breaker ribbon for a first speech (and was earlier a member of NOVA Toastmasters in Falls Church, VA), a member of American Legion Post 1 in Denver, and a member of Guitars for Veterans. Jayce served as an instructor with the U.S. Naval Sea Cadet Corps (Alamo Battalion, San Antonio, 2023), volunteered with the Phil Grove Big Dog Program in Austin (2022), and was a range safety officer at the Quantico Shooters Club (2020).

## Background and interests
Jayce graduated from Annandale High School in Fairfax County, Virginia (2008). Outside work Jayce runs a multi-node Ollama homelab for local LLM experiments. Jayce is an alumnus of Alpha Sigma Lambda (adult-learner academic honor society), the IMA Accounting Honor Society, and Lambda Phi Epsilon fraternity (Virginia Commonwealth University chapter, Theta Class).

## Volunteering and hobbies
Jayce has volunteered with Habitat for Humanity on a build for veterans. Outside work Jayce bikes the Rocky Mountain backcountry in Colorado, has ridden BMX in Philadelphia, has visited Griffith Observatory in Los Angeles. Jayce is a car enthusiast who drives a built 2017 Subaru STI. Jayce is a fan of the anime and manga One Piece and collects One Piece trading cards. Jayce graduated from the University of Maryland in 2019 (B.S. in Accounting, University of Maryland Global Campus).
