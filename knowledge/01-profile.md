## How Jayce builds AI systems
Jayce's engineering approach comes from the flight line, where a 7-level signs for every job against technical data before an aircraft flies: nothing leaves the shop without a sign-off. Jayce builds AI systems the same way, measured, traceable and honest about what they don't know, around three principles. Ground every answer: retrieval over the real source material (regulations, protocols, patient records) with citations required, and the system says so when the sources don't cover a question instead of guessing; the TTB label verification tool and the clinical co-pilot work this way. Gate every release: versioned prompts, golden datasets, regression replay and canary rollouts with automatic rollback, so a change ships when the evals say so, not when the demo looks good; this is the release process Jayce uses at Teos. Secure by default: PHI de-identified at ingest, tenant-scoped retrieval, audit logs, authentication on every endpoint and STIG-hardened hosts, a habit from six years of NIST and RMF work on federal cloud systems.
