## Service overview
Jayce served 16 years across three components: the U.S. Marine Corps Reserve (2008-2014, Sergeant), the D.C. Air National Guard (2014-2022, Staff Sergeant), and the U.S. Air Force Reserve (2021-2024, Staff Sergeant). Jayce is a veteran of the United States Armed Forces.

## U.S. Marine Corps (2008-2014)
Recruit training with Platoon 3010, Quebec Company, 3rd Recruit Training Battalion, Parris Island, SC; Marine Combat Training at the School of Infantry, Camp Lejeune, NC; then the 1316 Metal Worker MOS course at Aberdeen Proving Ground, MD (welding: oxy-acetylene, stick, flux-cored, GTAW/TIG, MIG). Based at Marine Corps Base Quantico, VA, Jayce was promoted to Lance Corporal (2009), Corporal (2012) and Sergeant (2014), serving as Tactical Welding Shop Chief and Infantry Training NCO. Jayce took part in the "Shared Accord" exercise in South Africa (2011) and earned the Rifle Expert badge twice.

## D.C. Air National Guard - C-40 propulsion (2014-2022)
Aerospace propulsion (jet engine) mechanic, 7-level, 213th Maintenance Squadron at Joint Base Andrews, MD. As a C-40C propulsion shop maintenance NCO Jayce supported government diplomatic and distinguished-visitor missions out of Andrews, including White House missions, and helped manage mission-essential components for more than $2 billion in Air Force aviation assets. Training included the 424-hour TF34 jet engine course at Sheppard AFB, GE CFM56-7 line maintenance and borescope inspector courses, Boeing 737/7X7 maintenance and human-factors courses, C-40B/C engine and APU operator courses, and Airman Leadership School. Jayce received the Golden Bolt Award (213th MXS, 2016).

## U.S. Air Force Reserve - C-5M Galaxy propulsion (2021-2024)
Aerospace propulsion craftsman (7-level, Staff Sergeant) with the 433rd Airlift Wing in San Antonio, TX, maintaining engines on the C-5M Galaxy, the largest airframe in the Air Force inventory. Jayce led a propulsion shop: inspection, troubleshooting, engine removal and installation, engine run-up, and airworthiness documentation. Jayce held 7-level sign-off authority over work released from the shop, verified technical data compliance, sequenced and assigned jobs across the crew, and trained 3- and 5-level airmen to qualification standard.

## Decorations
Air and Space Achievement Medal (2019), Army Commendation Medal (2017), Humanitarian Service Medal (2018), Air and Space Outstanding Unit Award (2018), Global War on Terrorism Service Medal, National Defense Service Medal, Selected Marine Corps Reserve Medal, Air Force Longevity Service Award, NCO Professional Military Education Graduate Ribbon, and Small Arms Expert Marksmanship Ribbon.

## How service shapes Jayce's engineering
On the flight line, nothing leaves the shop without a qualified sign-off against technical data. Jayce applies the same discipline to AI systems: eval gates and regression harnesses before release, audit logs, human review when confidence is low, and refusing to guess when the source material doesn't cover a question.
